About Me

Welcome!

My name is Agnes Kocsis. I graduated in Economics from Corvinus University and worked for nearly 17 years in the financial sector of a multinational corporation. Numbers, systems, and precision have always been close to me — but over time I realized that personal interaction with people and the joy of creating a home are much more aligned with my true passion. In 2024 I began my career as a real estate agent.

Today I work independently as a freelance real estate agent. Collaborating with other equally independent professionals, I ensure that my clients receive the highest level of attention and support — since I take on only a few assignments at a time, I can devote real time and energy to each one.

Accuracy, clear communication, and efficiency are core values for me. I firmly believe that correct and transparent collaboration forms the best foundation for a successful real estate transaction.

If you have a property for sale, or if any of our listings have caught your interest, please do not hesitate to contact me — I would be delighted to help you achieve your goals.
